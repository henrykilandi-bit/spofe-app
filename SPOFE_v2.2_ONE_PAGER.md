## 💡 Key Decisions Required

### 1. GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY
**Decision:** Internal team or hire VP Sales?
- **Option A**: Hire experienced VP Sales ($150-200K salary)
- **Option B**: Promote from within (if capable)
- **Option C**: Use consulting firm for first 6 months

**Recommendation**: **Hire external VP Sales**  
*Why: Needs outside market perspective, sales experience, credibility*

### 2. PRIVATE BETA APPROACH
**Decision:** Who are the first 20 customers?
- **Option A**: Existing network/relationships
- **Option B**: Outbound sales team (hire interim)
- **Option C**: Warm introductions through advisors

**Recommendation**: **Mix A + C** (network + advisors)  
*Why: Fastest, lowest friction, best feedback*

### 3. PRICING STRATEGY
**Decision:** Freemium, freemium+, or premium only?
- **Option A**: Freemium (free tier + paid upgrades)
- **Option B**: Free trial + paid ($50-500/month)
- **Option C**: Premium only ($200-1000/month)

**Recommendation**: **Option B** (free trial + tiered pricing)  
*Why: Proven SaaS model, lowers sales friction, highest conversion*

### 4. GEOGRAPHIC FOCUS
**Decision:** Which countries first?
- **Option A**: Senegal only (home market, fastest)
- **Option B**: Francophone Africa (5 countries, wider market)
- **Option C**: Pan-African (20+ countries, complex)

**Recommendation**: **Option B** (Francophone Africa)  
*Why: Natural market extension, OHADA compliance shared, manageable complexity*

### 5. FUNDING STRATEGY
**Decision:** Bootstrapped, angel, or Series A now?
- **Option A**: Bootstrapped (18-24 months, slow)
- **Option B**: Angel round ($300-500K, 6-12 months)
- **Option C**: Series A now (risky, unproven)

**Recommendation**: **Option B** (Angel round post-beta)  
*Why: De-risk with beta validation, fund growth, avoid premature Series A*
